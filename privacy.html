CreatorFlow Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CreatorFlow handles creator authentication information, local video content, publishing data, and interactions with supported third-party platforms.

Last updated: August 29, 2026

1. About CreatorFlow

CreatorFlow is a desktop creator utility designed to help authenticated creators review, manage, and publish their own video content through supported platform integrations.

CreatorFlow uses official APIs and authorization mechanisms provided by connected platforms.

2. Information CreatorFlow May Access

When a creator connects a supported account, CreatorFlow may receive information necessary to provide the requested publishing functionality.

  • Basic authorized account information
  • Account identifiers required by official platform APIs
  • Available publishing and privacy settings
  • Authentication tokens required for authorized API access
  • Publishing status information for submitted content

3. Authentication Information

CreatorFlow may store access tokens and refresh tokens locally on the creator's device so the application can perform actions that the creator has authorized.

Authentication credentials are used only for approved application functionality and should not be shared publicly.

4. Video Content

CreatorFlow processes video files selected or prepared by the creator for publishing.

Local video files remain under the creator's control. CreatorFlow sends a video to a connected platform only as part of an authorized publishing workflow.

5. TikTok Integration

CreatorFlow may integrate with TikTok using official TikTok developer products, including Login Kit and the Content Posting API.

TikTok may independently process information submitted through its APIs according to TikTok's own terms, developer policies, and privacy practices.

6. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by CreatorFlow may be used to:

  • Authenticate the creator
  • Display connected-account information
  • Retrieve available creator publishing settings
  • Upload creator-selected content
  • Check publishing and processing status
  • Recover safely from interrupted publishing operations

7. Data Sharing

CreatorFlow does not sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to connected services when required to perform an action explicitly authorized through CreatorFlow, such as authenticating an account or publishing selected content.

8. Data Storage

CreatorFlow is designed as a desktop application. Application state, authentication information, and publishing records may be stored locally on the creator's device.

Creators remain responsible for maintaining the security of their computer and local application files.

9. Data Retention

Locally stored application data may remain on the creator's device until it is removed by the creator or no longer required by the application.

Data processed by connected third-party platforms may be subject to those platforms' own retention policies.

10. Creator Control

Creators control which supported accounts they authorize and which content they choose to submit through CreatorFlow.

Authorization may be revoked through the connected platform where supported.

11. Security

CreatorFlow is designed to use official authentication and API mechanisms instead of collecting platform passwords directly.

Reasonable technical measures are used to reduce accidental disclosure of locally stored authentication information.

12. Third-Party Services

CreatorFlow depends on external services and APIs for some functionality. These services operate independently and may have their own terms, privacy policies, limitations, and availability.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated as CreatorFlow's functionality or integrations change.

The most recent version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

14. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the CreatorFlow project repository or official project channels.

CreatorFlow is intended to help creators manage and publish their own content through authorized platform integrations.
